Method

Designed with research. Delivered through conversation.

Seyyahname builds on three international frameworks: the Council of Europe's CEFR Companion Volume (2020), the ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines and SuperMemo's SM-2. At the center of it all is Krashen's principle of comprehensible input.

CEFR CV 2020 ACTFL 2024 SM-2 Krashen i+1
  1. 01

    i+1: one step above your level

    Your coach tunes its own English to your level: short, clear sentences at A1, nuanced and idiomatic language at C1. Every reply slips in a structure from the next band, in natural context.

  2. 02

    Triple-Layer Feedback

    Every answer gets three passes:

    • 💪 Strength

      What you did right or bravely, quoted back.

    • 🔁 Recast

      Your sentence, corrected and natural.

    • ✨ Polish

      How a native speaker would put it.

  3. 03

    Evidence-based level-ups

    You earn a Can-Do goal by scoring 80% or higher on three different tasks, confirmed by the feedback. Complete a band's four goals and the next band unlocks. No level is ever skipped on a guess.

  4. 04

    A radar for Turkish-speaker pitfalls

    It spots patterns carried over from Turkish, like "I have 30 years", "listen music" or "since 3 years", logs them, and gently follows up until you use the right form three sessions in a row.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Ask your coach inside the app. It's happy to help.

Seyyahname is built around real communication, not gamified word matching. Every sentence you write or say is assessed against CEFR Can-Do descriptors, refined with three-layer feedback, and the words you learn move into long-term memory with the SM-2 algorithm. You practise to actually speak, not to collect points.

i+1 says you acquire a language fastest from input one step above your current level: too easy and you stop growing, too hard and you stall. Seyyahname tunes every reply to that sweet spot. Triple-Layer Feedback then handles each answer in three passes: what you did well (Strength), a natural corrected version of your sentence (Recast), and the phrasing that takes you to the next level (Polish).

Your first tasks set your starting level. From there, concrete Can-Do goals are tracked across 12 sub-bands from A1 to C2. You earn a goal after succeeding at 80% or higher on three different tasks, confirmed by the feedback; once every goal in a band is done, the next band unlocks. Speaking, writing, reading and listening are tracked separately.
